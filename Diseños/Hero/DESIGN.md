## Brand & Style

This design system manifests the intersection of high-order technical engineering and tactile, avant-garde editorial publishing. Built for sophisticated portfolio narratives, architectural technologists, and creative directors, it balances cold algorithmic precision with humanistic sensibility.

### Design Principles
- **Architectural Scaffolding:** Interface boundaries are demarcated by fine hairline structural grids, technical metadata callouts, and modular alignments rather than heavy drop shadows.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Rhythmic tension between heavy display titles, intentional dead whitespace, and tight data-dense monospaced clusters creates an intentional, magazine-grade layout hierarchy.
- **Instrument Precision:** Interactive elements respond with razor-sharp micro-interactions, distinct hover states, and monospaced technical metadata (timestamps, coordinates, commit indexes).
- **Material Restraint:** Color is deployed as an intentional editorial highlighter rather than decorative fill. Contrast is crisp and balanced across tactile paper hues in light mode and deep obsidian twilight in dark mode.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intellectual, tactile reading environment inspired by heavy uncoated archival paper, blueprint ink, and warm raw umber annotations.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas (`#F6F5F1` / Dark: `#101A25`):** Raw, unbleached tactile canvas in light mode; deep architectural navy-slate in dark mode.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` / Dark: `#1C2A38`):** Elevated structural sheets, cards, and modal backdrops.
- **Primary Ink (`#183044` / Dark: `#F0F3F5`):** Midnight Prussian blue acting as the primary typography and high-emphasis structure.
- **Secondary Accent (`#A07650` / Dark: `#D5AE82`):** Raw umber and bronze-tinted technical callout tone for dates, badges, focus states, and editorial highlights.
- **Muted / Subtext (`#526372` / Dark: `#A9B7C4`):** Mid-tone slate for supporting descriptions, secondary metadata, and secondary navigation.
- **Hairlines & Boundaries (`#D9DFE2` / Dark: `#34485A`):** Low-contrast architectural hair dividers (1px) used to partition sections and maintain crisp modular structures.

## Typography

Typography delivers the core duality of this design system:
- **Display & Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric, geometric-grotesque authority with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) evoking contemporary broadsheet publications and avant-garde monographs.
- **Long-form Narrative & Body (Inter):** Highly legible, humanized neutral sans-serif designed for structural comfort in essays, technical case studies, and analytical logs.
- **Instrument & Metadata (JetBrains Mono):** Upper-cased indices, dates, section tags, git coordinates, and architectural indexes set with generous tracking (`0.08em`) to enforce engineering clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical 12-column architectural grid punctuated by visible continuous hairline boundaries.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Configuration:** 
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, 40px outer margins, 40px gutters. Typographic layouts often offset content across 7-to-5 or 8-to-4 asymmetrical column spans to mirror high-end editorial magazines.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 40px margins, 24px gutters.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px margins, 16px gutters.
- **Pacing & Rhythm:** Generous vertical breaks (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) isolate case studies, while technical metadata utilizes dense clustering (`space-2xs` to `space-sm`) inside hairline containers.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, blurred consumer drop shadows, employing physical layering, tonal contrast, and technical offset planes instead.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Tonal Plane Stacking:** Elevation is established by placing elevated `#FFFFFF` / `#1C2A38` panels over `#F6F5F1` / `#101A25` backdrops.
- **Precision Hairline Dividers:** 1px solid borders (`#D9DFE2` in light, `#34485A` in dark) define boundaries between all panels, cards, header tracks, and sidebar navigations.
- **Architectural Drop Shift:** Key interactive cards and floating tooltips use an intentional 1px solid hairline frame combined with a sharp, hard-edge 2px or 4px unblurred offset shadow in `#183044` (or dark mode equivalent) upon active hover, resembling drafting paper overlays.
- **Translucent Overlays:** Overlay drawers and command palettes utilize a crisp 85% opacity surface with an 8px backdrop blur, bounded by a 1px border.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a strict, sharp geometric profile (`roundedness: 0`). 

Corners are 0px across containers, buttons, tags, input surfaces, and modals. This deliberate absence of rounded corners honors raw architectural blueprints, Swiss graphic design traditions, and computational terminal geometry. Visual softness is introduced strictly through generous typographic spacing and gentle tonal canvas warmth rather than curved radii.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid background (`#183044` in light, `#F0F3F5` in dark), inverse typography (`#F6F5F1` or `#101A25`), 0px border radius, uppercase `technical-label` or `body-sm` font. Padding: `12px 24px`. Hover triggers a crisp inversion or slight background shift to `#A07650`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a 1px hairline border (`#D9DFE2` / `#34485A`) and primary ink typography. Hover applies surface fill (`#FFFFFF` / `#1C2A38`).
- **Monospace Text Action:** Bare link paired with a rightward directional arrow (`→`), prefixed by a numeric index (`[01] Read Case Study`), shifting to accent `#A07650` on hover.

### Chips & Technical Badges
- Sharp, rectangular containers with 1px hairline borders.
- Typography: `technical-label`, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.08em`.
- Padding: `4px 8px`.
- Status indicators feature a 6px square glyph (accent `#A07650` or active green `#2E7D32`) rather than a circular dot.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs & Textareas:** Framed in a 1px continuous border with surface background. Focus states transition the border to `#183044` (light) or `#D5AE82` (dark) with a 1px inset rule—no diffuse focus glows. Floating labels utilize `technical-label`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square geometries (0px radius). Checked state displays a solid fill with an internal negative cutout or square indicator.

### Structural Cards & Project Dossiers
- Framed in 1px hairline borders with zero radius.
- Composed of modular partitions: a top metadata header bar (displaying project date, category, and technical index in `JetBrains Mono`), an image or diagram bay, and a narrative body zone separated by interior 1px horizontal hairline dividers.

### Lists & Archival Tables
- Row items separated by 1px horizontal hairline rules.
- On hover, row surfaces illuminate with a subtle tint (`#FFFFFF` in light mode, `#1C2A38` in dark mode).
- Flexible three-column layout: left column contains monospace timestamp/ID, center contains the project headline, right contains category tag and external link indicator.

### Additional Editorial Components
- **Index Headers:** Page headers accompanied by architectural numbering (e.g., `SECTION // 04.1`), providing technical structure to case study transitions.
- **Pull Quotes:** Left-aligned statement in `headline-md` bounded by a 2px vertical hairline accent line in `#A07650`.
- **Blueprint Spec Blocks:** Monospaced data readouts framed in low-contrast boxes for stack architecture, project metrics, and client metadata.
